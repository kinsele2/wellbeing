# Diet Baseline

**Period covered**: Approximately January–April of this year (best recent dietary period)
> [!NOTE]
> This represents my demonstrated achievable best, not an ideal. Habits deteriorated from around start of April and had largely reverted by mid-May when we left for holidays.

---

## Breakfast (Every Day, Monday–Sunday)

- Sugary cereal — usually Cornflakes or Weetabix, occasionally Muesli
- One slice of toast with real butter (intentional — to aid absorption of daily omega 3 supplement)
- Omega 3 supplement taken with this meal
- 500ml water
- One mug of coffee

**Notes**: The butter with toast is deliberate for supplement absorption. The cereal choice is a known problem — high in sugar, likely contributing to blood sugar cycling and energy crashes.

---

## Lunch — Working From Home Days (Mon, Wed, most Fridays)

Typically one of:

- Tomato wrap with hummus spread, tomatoes, cucumber, grilled lamb sticks, lettuce (eaten on roughly 2 of the 3 days)
- Simple sandwich — ham and cheese on white bread, maybe lettuce

---

## Lunch — Office Days (Tue, Thu)

- Whatever sandwich is available at the office — typically chicken, lettuce, tomato, with a mayonnaise-based sauce
- Limited control over these choices

---

## Lunch — Weekends

Typically one of:

- Scrambled egg on toast or waffles
- Sausages and black pudding with toast
- Roughly once a month: a breakfast roll (egg, sausage, pudding in a bap)

---

## Dinners — Weekly Rotation

The following represent a typical week's dinner rotation. Most are home cooked.

**Tex Mex Chicken Stir Fry**
Butcher-purchased pre-marinated chicken pieces in a mildly spicy sauce, with green beans, peppers, onions. Served with boiled rice. Fried. Not heavily vegetable-laden but some veg present.

**Oven Lasagna and Chips**
Pre-made oven lasagna with oven chips and mushy peas.

**Spaghetti Bolognese** *(batch cooked, covers 2–3 dinners)*
Home made: minced beef, peppers, carrots, broccoli, Uncle Ben's or similar jar sauce, spaghetti. Batch cooked. This is a high-frequency meal and a reliable one.

**Chicken Kievs**
Oven-cooked chicken kievs with boiled potatoes, mushy peas or beans, and sometimes carrots and broccoli.

**Burgers and Chips**
Butcher-purchased burgers, oven chips.

**Meatballs in Sauce with Rice**
Butcher-purchased meatballs cut into pieces, served with rice, carrots, and broccoli.

**Chicken and Veg with Rice** *(less frequent, roughly alternates with bolognese)*
Diced chicken breasts (butcher), peppers, carrots, broccoli, jar sauce, served with rice.

**Weekend Takeaway** *(approximately 50% of weekends)*
- Most commonly: chicken biryani from a Pakistani restaurant
- Alternatively: Thai green chicken curry (minimal vegetables)

## Snacks — Good Weeks

- Unsalted mixed nuts
- NAKD bars (vegan compressed fruit/nut bars — most commonly Cocoa Orange flavour)
- Fruit: bananas occasionally, approximately 1 per week (very low overall fruit intake)

## Snacks — Poor Weeks

- Chocolate binge eating (e.g. entire Easter egg in one sitting)
- Haribo-type sweets
- Sugary treats available in office (doughnuts, etc.)

> [!NOTE]
> Snacking pattern has an all-or-nothing quality — either controlled or a significant binge. This is worth tracking.

---

## Drinks

**Daily**
- 2 cups of coffee (second cup no later than 3pm)
- Multiple cups of tea (several per day, minimum)
- Water: minimum 2 pints daily; 4 pints on running days

**Alcohol**
- Good weeks: approximately 5 pints of beer, usually IPAs (e.g. O'Hara's Green IPA, Rascals)
- Not-so-good weeks: approximately 8 pints
- Roughly once every 2 months: a social occasion involving approximately 8 pints in one evening, usually with one separate evening of approximately 2 pints in the same week
- The solo 2-pint evenings (typically while gaming) are intentional and serve a genuine regulatory/decompression function

---

## Known Problem Areas

1. **Breakfast cereal** — high sugar, likely causing blood sugar cycling
2. **Alcohol** — volume is the single biggest dietary lever for cholesterol
3. **Low soluble fibre** — very little oats, lentils, beans, or fruit beyond occasional bananas; soluble fibre is a key cholesterol-management dietary tool
4. **Low fruit intake** — approximately 1 banana per week is very low
5. **Saturated fat accumulation** — butter daily, chicken kievs, chips, ready lasagna
6. **Poor week binge eating** — sugary snacks consumed in large quantities in all-or-nothing pattern
7. **No fallback position** — when weekly shop doesn't happen, diet collapses to ready meals and takeaways with no intermediate option
8. **Office lunch** — limited control, tends toward higher-calorie mayonnaise-heavy options

---

## What Is Working

- Home-cooked dinners are genuinely reasonable — bolognese, stir fry, meatball rice dish all contain real food and some vegetables
- Butcher sourcing rather than supermarket pre-packaged meat
- Batch cooking the bolognese (structural habit worth preserving and expanding)
- Unsalted nuts and NAKD bars as snacks on good weeks
- Water intake is adequate
- Omega 3 and CBD supplementation daily
- Good coffee discipline (no caffeine after 3pm)

# Tesco Click & Collect — Weekly Basket

**Purpose**: A default weekly order that can be placed online with minimal decision-making, covering most dinners, all breakfasts, lunches, and snacks. Designed to remove the friction of in-store shopping while supporting the dietary changes in the 8-week plan.

**How to use**: Save this as a reusable list on tesco.ie. Each week, place the order with minor edits as needed (e.g. skip something you still have). Collect on a fixed day — ideally Sunday or Monday to set up the week.

> [!NOTE]
> Butcher purchases (chicken pieces, meatballs, mince, burgers, chicken breasts, lamb sticks) are kept separate and assumed to continue as-is. This basket covers everything else.

## What This Basket Does

- Covers 5–6 dinners (combined with butcher purchases)
- Covers all breakfasts for the week
- Covers WFH lunches (office days are separate)
- Provides fallback meals requiring minimal cooking
- Adds frozen spinach as an almost invisible nutritional boost to existing dishes
- Swaps sugary cereal for oats
- Upgrades white bread to wholegrain where possible
- Replaces mushy peas with frozen peas
- Provides controlled sweet snack option to reduce binge risk

**Approximate weekly spend estimate**: €80–€100 for this basket, separate from butcher. Subject to variation.

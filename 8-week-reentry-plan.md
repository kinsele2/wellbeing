# 8-Week Re-Entry Plan — Diet & Exercise

**Period**: 8 working weeks from return home (before next 2-week break)
**Framing**: This is not 8 weeks to fix everything. It is 8 weeks to prove the system works — to demonstrate that the structural changes are sustainable for you and your family. The target at week 8 is not perfection. It is: the shopping system is holding, the breakfast swap has stuck, the exercise routine is largely back, and you feel measurably better than you did on the day you returned from holidays.

---

## The Two Phases

### Phase 1 — Weeks 1–2: Re-entry and Reset

The body needs time to readjust after a holiday break. Attempting the full April routine from day one will cause significant muscle soreness, feel punishing, and risk early dropout. These two weeks are about reactivating the system, not hitting targets.

**Exercise — Weeks 1–2**

| Day | Session | Notes |
|-----|---------|-------|
| Tuesday | Pilates | Attempt even if motivation is low — 30 minutes, non-negotiable effort |
| Tuesday | BodyPump | Priority anchor — do this even if nothing else happens |
| Thursday | 4–5km treadmill run (easy pace) | Reduced distance, reduced pressure |

- Monday run is not in the plan for week 1. Adding it in week 2 is fine if the body is responding well.
- BodyPump soreness after week 1 is unavoidable and expected — do not interpret this as something being wrong.
- The goal for week 1 is simply: attended BodyPump, did one run, attempted Pilates. That is a successful week.

**Diet — Weeks 1–2**

Focus on two changes only. Do not attempt to overhaul everything simultaneously.

1. **Breakfast swap**: Porridge every morning instead of sugary cereal. Keep the toast, keep the butter, keep the omega 3 routine. Just swap the cereal. This is the only mandatory dietary change in week 1.
2. **Place the Tesco Click & Collect order**: Do this in the first 2–3 days after returning. Getting the weekly shop done immediately sets the structural foundation for everything else. Do not wait until the "right time."

Everything else in weeks 1–2 — alcohol, snacking, dinner quality — aim to return to April baseline levels, no better or worse. You are not regressing, you are resetting.

---

### Phase 2 — Weeks 3–8: Building

Once the foundation is stable (shopping happening, breakfast changed, exercise routine reactivated), introduce improvements progressively.

---

## Weekly Breakdown

### Week 1 — Reactivate

**Exercise target**
- Pilates (Tuesday) ✓
- BodyPump (Tuesday) ✓
- 1 treadmill run, 5km easy (Thursday) ✓

**Diet target**
- Porridge every breakfast — no exceptions
- Tesco Click & Collect order placed and collected
- Everything else: April baseline

**Measure of success**: BodyPump attended. Order placed. Porridge happening.

---

### Week 2 — Stabilise

**Exercise target**
- Pilates (Tuesday) ✓
- BodyPump (Tuesday) ✓
- Treadmill run (Monday or Thursday) — return to 7km if comfortable, otherwise stay at 5km ✓

**Diet target**
- Porridge continues
- Second weekly shop placed (proving the order habit, not just a one-off)
- Begin replacing mushy peas with frozen peas where applicable

**Measure of success**: Two weeks of consistent BodyPump. Porridge still happening. Second order placed.

---

### Week 3 — Full Routine Returns

**Exercise target**
- Monday 8km treadmill run ✓
- Tuesday Pilates ✓
- Tuesday BodyPump ✓
- Thursday 7km treadmill run ✓

This is the full April routine. The goal from week 3 onward is to maintain it, not extend it.

**Diet target**
- Add frozen spinach to bolognese or rice dishes (small handful — test palatability)
- Increase fruit: aim for 1 banana or piece of fruit per day (up from ~1 per week)
- Begin reducing beer by approximately 1 pint per week from current baseline

**Measure of success**: Full routine completed at least 3 of 4 sessions. Fruit intake visibly higher than baseline.

---

### Week 4 — Consolidate

**Exercise target**
- Full April routine maintained
- Note any sessions missed and why — look for the pattern (is it Pilates first again?)

**Diet target**
- Alcohol: target 4 pints maximum this week (good week target, not emergency restriction)
- Try oatcakes with hummus as a snack replacement for one biscuit/sweet snack occasion
- Assess: is the spinach in bolognese acceptable? If yes, continue. If not, try adding to rice dishes instead.

**Measure of success**: Alcohol at or below 4 pints. Full routine held. Shopping order placed for fourth consecutive week.

---

### Week 5 — First Real Assessment

This is the halfway point. Take stock honestly.

**Questions to ask at week 5 check-in:**
- Is the shopping system holding? If not, what broke?
- Is porridge still happening every morning?
- Is the exercise routine at the April level or close to it?
- Is energy or motivation noticeably different from week 1?
- Has any weight change been noticed (not a primary goal, but a useful signal)?

**Exercise target**: Full routine. If Pilates has been the weak point, actively protect it this week.

**Diet target**
- Consider adding a second piece of fruit per day (apple or pear both contain soluble fibre relevant to cholesterol)
- If bolognese spinach is established, consider adding red lentils to the next bolognese batch (blend in — effectively invisible, significantly boosts soluble fibre and protein)

**Measure of success**: Honest assessment completed. Porridge unbroken. Shopping unbroken.

---

### Week 6 — Extend

**Exercise target**
- Full routine
- Optional: add a second BodyPump session if energy levels are good and motivation is there. Do not add if it feels like pressure.

**Diet target**
- Alcohol: maintain 4 pint target on normal weeks
- Assess snacking pattern: has the all-or-nothing binge dynamic reduced? Is the dark chocolate option being used as a controlled alternative?
- Try Greek yoghurt as a snack or breakfast alternative on one day

**Measure of success**: Full routine. Snacking pattern improving or at least stable.

---

### Week 7 — Reinforce

**Exercise target**: Full routine. Note: this is 6 weeks of consistent exercise. This is significant.

**Diet target**
- Assess breakfast: has porridge become habitual (i.e. not something requiring decision-making each morning)?
- If fruit intake is now daily, consider whether any other April-era gap needs addressing
- One dinner this week: try a simple lentil-based meal (red lentil soup or lentil bolognese variation) — high soluble fibre, cholesterol-relevant, family-friendly

**Measure of success**: Porridge now habitual. Exercise routine feels normal rather than effortful to initiate.

---

### Week 8 — Review Before Break

The final week before the next 2-week break. This week has two purposes: completing the 8-week run, and preparing the system to survive a second holiday period without a full collapse.

**Exercise target**: Full routine. Finish the 8 weeks with a complete week.

**Diet target**
- No new changes this week — consolidate what is working
- Place a Tesco order before leaving that covers the first week back, so re-entry after break 2 is faster than re-entry after break 1

**End-of-8-week review questions:**
- What changed that stuck?
- What didn't stick, and why?
- What is the minimum viable routine to maintain during the next break (i.e. some walking, some movement — not full training, but not complete inactivity)?
- What does week 1 of the next 8-week block look like?

---

## Minimum Viable Week (for disrupted weeks)

When a week goes wrong — illness, work pressure, family demands — this is the floor. Holding this is not failure, it is intelligent adaptation.

| Element | Minimum |
|---------|---------|
| Exercise | BodyPump only |
| Breakfast | Porridge |
| Shopping | Place the order even if it's a reduced basket |
| Alcohol | Do not exceed 8 pints |

Any week that holds this minimum is a week that didn't break the habit. That matters more than any individual week being perfect.

---

## Tracking Suggestions

For each weekly check-in, note the following simply:

- **Exercise**: sessions completed out of target (e.g. 3/4)
- **Porridge**: days completed out of 7
- **Shopping order**: placed yes/no
- **Alcohol**: approximate units
- **Energy/motivation**: 1–5 subjective rating
- **Notable events**: anything that disrupted the week

This gives enough data to spot patterns without becoming a burden to maintain.

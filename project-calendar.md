## The Lower Volume Week

Reduced volume, never a stop. Tuesday stays fully intact in every one.

| Day | Session |
|---|---|
| Monday | Rest |
| Tuesday | Unchanged - Pilates, BodyPump, Session A |
| Thursday | 5k easy run, Session B |

Diet, alcohol, porridge, shopping: unchanged. Training changes are decoupled
from dietary changes.

**Why the cut lands on the runs, not on Tuesday:**

- The fatigue load is in the 15k of running, not in Tuesday
- Cutting BodyPump also cuts Session A, which is anchored to it
- BodyPump absence produces soreness on return, and that return is always the
  first week of a full block
- Tuesday carries three of five weekly touchpoints and is the most expensive
  thing in the project to restart
